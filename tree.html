The ban phase as a game

Why not just ban the hero with the best win rate? A win rate mixes the hero with the players who choose it, a ban changes what both teams draft, and the other team answers your bans without showing who they are. So Ban Call plays the ban phase out as a game with hidden types and solves it backwards.
