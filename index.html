Choosing a ban is harder than it looks. A ban removes a hero from both teams, it changes what both teams draft, and you usually can't see who is on the other side. Ban Call plays your lobby out many times before you choose. The two figures show how, with the model's own numbers for the lobby set above: loading.

Where a branch ends, all six bans are in. The seats you can't see are filled with real players from your rank, everyone picks, and every lineup is scored against every opposing one. This repeats at every ending.
