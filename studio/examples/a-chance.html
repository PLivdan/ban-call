How much each ban is worth, run by runaverage and chance it is best

Each curve is the spread of one ban's value across the simulated ban phases: the rest of the bans, the stand-in players and both teams' drafts are drawn again every run, and the same draws are used for every ban. Blue is where the ban helps your team, orange where it hurts. The tick is the average and the black bar under it is its 95% interval: the average is known far more precisely than any single run. Curves that overlap this much are why no single ban wins most runs.
