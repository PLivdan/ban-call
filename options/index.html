Main page options

Real numbers from the live v8.3 model for one lobby. Pick one of each (or mix). Nothing here is on the main page yet.

Your win chance

Right now the page says it in one grey line: Win chance 52.9% if you follow the advice · 51.7% if both teams ban as usual. The honest size of a ban's effect is about a point or two of win chance, so each option makes that visible without inflating it.

W1

Scoreboard: the number as the headline

For: impossible to miss, sits next to the ban advice, the gain is the chip. Against: a big 53% can read as a promise; the scale bar keeps it honest.

W2

Tug of war: your share of the match

For: shows both teams at once, and the zoomed strip makes a one-point swing visible. Against: at full width the gain is a sliver, which is true but can feel like nothing.

W3

Ban phase timeline: where the edge comes from

For: shows where the edge comes from: your advised bans step it up, theirs barely move it. It updates as bans are entered. Against: the most to read; it needs the whole width.

W4

Out of 100 games like this

For: the plainest way to say what a point is worth: one more win in about a hundred games. Against: small effects look small, because they are.

Likely openers

Right now: a mirrored bar chart of hero names, from the previous model, hidden in a closed section. All four options use the v8 simulator's drafts of this lobby ( simulated ban phases, the rest of the bans as typical teams make them), so they match the model that gives the advice. On the page that means one background run of a couple of seconds.

O1

By role, portraits sized by chance

For: reads like a draft screen, and role balance is visible at a glance. Against: shows the top four per role only.

O2

Their likely comp: role split, then each slot

For: answers "what are we playing into" in one line (2-2-2 in most drafts), then the likeliest hero in each slot with the next two. Against: the slots are the likeliest per role, not one real lineup: no single lineup comes up in more than a fraction of a percent of drafts.

O3

The whole roster with two bars per hero

For: every hero in one place, in the same order as the hero picker; heroes likely to be banned fade out. Against: dense; the eye has to hunt for the long bars.

O4

The current mirror, done properly

For: the smallest change: portraits in the middle, the ten likeliest, bars on both sides. Against: still a list, with no sense of roles or comps.
